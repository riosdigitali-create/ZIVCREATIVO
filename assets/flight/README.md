# Recursos de la intro orbital

Generados con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`, sin API ni CLI adicional. Los originales se conservan localmente, fuera del repositorio.

Recursos usados por la web:

- `assets/flight/cockpit.webp`: marco fotográfico de cabina, 1672 × 941, transparencia central conservada. Original: `exec-8acbb30c-5a10-407a-8bfc-aeaaaaad052e.png`. Convertido a WebP, sin cambios de composición.
- `assets/flight/cloud-density.webp`: campo de nubes, optimizado a 1024 × 1024 para su uso como textura en WebGL. Original: `exec-df85c9b0-9047-4009-af8c-72b8bfe2a46d.png`.

## Prompt de la cabina

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: transparent foreground overlay for an animated first-person spacecraft launch on a website. Create a photorealistic photograph-like view from the pilot seat of a real near-future orbital capsule. Landscape 16:9 composition. The entire enormous central windshield opening MUST be completely transparent alpha, with no glass tint and no scenery: the live animated view will be placed behind it. Keep 82 percent of the image open and transparent. Only render the spacecraft structure around the outermost edges: thin realistic graphite titanium windshield surround at the top and sides, heavier dark curved dashboard at the very bottom 13 percent, subtle tactile seams, brushed and machined metal, deeply recessed fasteners, rubber window gaskets, subtle blue illuminated strips, realistic bounce light and micro scratches. Upper edge only 5 percent thick, side pillars only 5 percent thick. No seats, people, hands, steering wheels, outer spacecraft, sky, stars or planet. Restrained elegant industrial engineering, a convincing physical spacecraft rather than a neon game HUD. No text, letters, logos or numbers. Dark navy-black materials, cold silver edge highlights and restrained ice-blue accents. Absolutely transparent center and all window space. Output one high quality wide overlay.

## Prompt de las nubes

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square cloud field texture for a cinematic real-time volumetric cloud shader. Frame filled edge to edge with natural dense cumulonimbus and cumulus cloud forms viewed from above at high altitude, very detailed large sculpted billows with fine wisps and natural atmospheric depth. Neutral monochrome grayscale: cloud tops range white to pale gray and deep pockets between clouds range charcoal gray to black. Dramatic soft sunlight from upper left shapes three-dimensional billows. No horizon, no blue sky, no land, no objects, no stars, no text, no borders or logos. A continuous organic field of clouds with several large masses and airy gaps, not striped, not flat noise, not fractal-looking. Photographic large-format atmospheric texture, high detail and realistic scattering. Seamless tileable appearance at the edges. One square grayscale texture.
